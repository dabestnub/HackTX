A little guide to getting in sync

  1. Invite your teammateCreate a room, choose your points goal and round length, then share the six-character code with a friend.
  2. Start the roundBoth players enable local ASL recognition. In visible mode, each player also chooses to share live video.
  3. Sign one letter at a timeFollow your word from left to right. Hold each sign steady for 1.5 seconds until the progress bar fills, then move to the next letter.
  4. Build something togetherYou each spell your own word at the same time. Finish both words to create a shared picture and keep earning team points.
